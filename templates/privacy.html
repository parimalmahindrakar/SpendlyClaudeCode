Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to authenticate you, to show your expenses and spending summaries, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers we control and is retained for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data and expense records.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
